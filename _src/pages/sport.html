Team Singapore, #5

From 2023 to 2025 I played for Singapore's men's national baseball team, including at the 19th Asian Games in Hangzhou and the 2023 East Asia Cup.

Hangzhou was something else. A huge, beautiful field, a stadium packed to the top rows, and a crowd so loud you could feel it. Competing in that atmosphere, against teams that good, is one of the most inspiring things I've ever done.

Before that: softball captain for my hall at NTU, where we finished in the top four of the inter-hall games; vice-captain at Hwa Chong, with a National School Games gold, a Colours Award (Distinction) and the Eagles Award for leadership and character; and at Catholic High, four Colours Awards (Distinction) and three National School Games golds.

What sport taught me, and what I brought to security

DisciplineTraining doesn't care how you feel that day. Neither does an engagement deadline.
Time managementTraining, school and work in the same day teaches you to plan every hour.
PrioritisingKnow which pitch to swing at. Most of the job is choosing what not to do.
ConsistencyA little every day, without stopping. That's how swings get built, and exploits too.
ResilienceYou strike out, you sit down, you go up again. Never give up on the at-bat.
RespectEveryone is strong in their own way. The best teams, on a field or a red team, know it.

Everything else with a ball or a net

I'll play almost anything: squash, table tennis, badminton, pickleball, softball and swimming, plus plenty of competitive sport for my hall. In the gym I train for functional strength first and looking good second.
